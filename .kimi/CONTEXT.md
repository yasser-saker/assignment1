# Business Context - Construction Takeoff Automation

## Project Type: Commercial Interior / TI (Tenant Improvement)

### Common Project Types
1. **Medical/Healthcare** (TAKEOFF-28: Maryland Vision Institute)
   - Rich in text: schedules, legends, specs
   - Equipment: HVAC (RTU, VAV, diffusers), Electrical (panels, receptacles)
   - Finishes: paint, ceiling tiles, flooring

2. **Government/Military** (TAKEOFF-50: Portland VA Surgical Center)
   - Large spec documents (1500+ pages)
   - Scanned architectural drawings (52 pages)
   - Finish codes: QT-01 (Quarry Tile), PT-01 (Porcelain Tile)

3. **Retail** (TAKEOFF-56: Jack & Jones Staten Island)
   - Millwork heavy: fitting rooms, cash counters, backwraps
   - Scanned pages with markups
   - Smaller scope (11 items)

## Construction Document Types

### Drawings
- **Floor Plans**: Room layouts, finish codes (graphics-based, hard to OCR)
- **Reflected Ceiling Plans**: Lighting locations, ceiling types
- **Demolition Plans**: What to remove
- **Details**: Construction details (cross-sections)
- **Schedules**: Equipment, finishes, doors (text-rich, easy to parse)
- **Legends**: Symbol explanations (text-rich)

### Specifications
- **CSI MasterFormat**: Division 01-49
  - Div 21: Fire Suppression
  - Div 22: Plumbing
  - Div 23: HVAC
  - Div 26: Electrical
  - Div 28: Electronic Safety & Security
  - Div 32: Exterior Improvements
- **Sections**: Part 1 (General), Part 2 (Products), Part 3 (Execution)

### Schedules Found in Drawings
- Diffuser/Grille Schedule
- VAV Terminal Unit Schedule
- RTU/Equipment Schedule
- Lighting Fixture Schedule
- Panel/Transformer Schedule
- Door Schedule
- Finish Legend
- Room Finish Schedule
