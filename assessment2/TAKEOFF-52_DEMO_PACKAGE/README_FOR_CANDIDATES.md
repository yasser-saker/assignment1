# Assessment 2.0 Challenge Files — TAKEOFF-52 Lovesac Corner Shoppes at Stadium

Use this folder for the 24-hour paid flooring takeoff challenge.

## What to upload into your app/product
Upload files from:

`01_INPUT_PROJECT_FILES_UPLOAD_THESE/`

These are the construction/project files your system should read using PDF + vision/OCR/layout extraction.

## Gold/human output
Use files from:

`02_HUMAN_GOLD_OUTPUT_FOR_SCORING_ONLY/`

Only use these AFTER your app generates its own prediction, to score/evaluate your result. Do not use these files as input during prediction.

## Expected workflow in your Loom
1. Open your app/product.
2. Upload the files from `01_INPUT_PROJECT_FILES_UPLOAD_THESE/`.
3. Run the flooring takeoff.
4. Show the generated marked drawing/PDF/image with measurement markers.
5. Show the generated Google Sheet/spreadsheet with measurements.
6. Compare your result against the human/gold output in `02_HUMAN_GOLD_OUTPUT_FOR_SCORING_ONLY/`.
7. Show whether you reached the 75% accuracy target.

## Important
We want to make the goal achievable. Use vision-capable AI/OCR/PDF tools. The app must actually read the PDF drawings and produce measurable flooring quantities with evidence.
